# Public Data Sources

## Objective

This section documents publicly available data sources that can support
Machine Learning applications in the Apparel & Textiles industry.

## 1. India Open Government Data

Source:
https://www.data.gov.in/

Potential data:

- Textile exports
- Apparel exports
- Country-wise exports
- State/UT-wise exports
- Category-wise exports

Potential ML applications:

- Demand analysis
- Trade forecasting
- Market analysis
- Regional analysis

---

## 2. Ministry of Textiles

Source:
https://texmin.nic.in/

Potential data:

- Textile industry statistics
- Apparel industry information
- Export statistics
- Government policies
- Sector reports

Potential applications:

- Market research
- Industry analysis
- Forecasting features

---

## 3. MoSPI / eSankhyiki

Source:
https://esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in/

Potential data:

- Manufacturing statistics
- Industrial indicators
- Economic statistics

Potential applications:

- Demand forecasting
- Manufacturing analysis
- Market forecasting

---

## 4. Google Trends

Source:
https://trends.google.com/

Potential data:

- Fashion search interest
- Product search trends
- Regional search interest
- Time-based search trends

Potential applications:

- Trend forecasting
- Consumer interest analysis
- Demand prediction

### Limitation

Google Trends represents search interest and should not be treated as
actual sales data.

---

https://huggingface.co/datasets/nreimers/fashion-dataset

---

## Source Selection Principles

The preferred sources should be:

1. Reliable
2. Publicly accessible
3. Relevant to the ML objective
4. Machine-readable where possible
5. Properly documented
6. Legally usable
7. Reproducible

## Important Rule

API keys, passwords, private customer information and restricted datasets
must never be uploaded to GitHub.
