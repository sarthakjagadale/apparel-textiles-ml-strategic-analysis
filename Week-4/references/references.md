## 6. Fashion-MNIST

Fashion-MNIST is a dataset containing grayscale images of fashion products. It is commonly used for image classification experiments.

The dataset contains 70,000 images belonging to 10 fashion categories.

It can be used to study:

- Image preprocessing
- Classification
- Neural Networks
- CNN-based approaches

Website:

https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist
